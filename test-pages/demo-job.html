Backend Engineer

Nimbus Robotics · Munich, Germany · Hybrid · €70,000–€95,000

About the role

Nimbus Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robots. We are hiring a Backend Engineer to own the fleet-coordination service that assigns tasks to several hundred robots in real time.

You will design APIs consumed by the robot firmware and the operations dashboard, harden the system against partial network failures, and keep p99 latency predictable as the fleet grows. Our stack is TypeScript and Go on Kubernetes, with PostgreSQL and NATS.

Requirements

  • Strong experience building and operating production backend services
  • Comfortable with distributed systems failure modes
  • Clear technical writing; English C1 or above
